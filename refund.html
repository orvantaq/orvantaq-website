Refund Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

At Orvantaq, we want customers to have a clear understanding of our refund policy before purchasing our digital products.

1. Refund Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 7 days of purchase in the following situations:

  • The product does not work as described.
  • The downloaded files are corrupted or incomplete.
  • A technical issue prevents the customer from using the product and cannot reasonably be resolved.

2. Non-Refundable Situations

Refund requests may be declined in situations such as:

  • The customer changes their mind after successfully downloading and using the product.
  • The issue results from an unsupported device or incompatible system.
  • The customer provides inaccurate information about the reported problem.

These conditions do not override any refund rights required by applicable law.

3. How to Request a Refund

Email us within 7 days of purchase with:

  • Your order number.
  • Date of purchase.
  • A description of the problem.

Contact: orvantaq.business@gmail.com

4. Review Process

Orvantaq will review refund requests individually. We may ask for additional information to investigate the reported issue and determine an appropriate resolution.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will generally be issued through the original payment method, subject to the payment provider's procedures and processing times.

6. Contact Us

For refund-related questions, contact: orvantaq.business@gmail.com
